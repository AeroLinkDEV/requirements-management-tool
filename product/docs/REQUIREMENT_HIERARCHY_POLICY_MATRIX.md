# Requirement-hierarchy policy-to-consumer matrix

Status: current characterization and readiness inventory through issue #706 for issues #702/#703/#704/#705/#706/#713 (Slice 6 of #697 plus configured-policy routing). This is an evidence artifact,
not a policy change. It records the behavior in the three-level catalogue and the project-owned effective ladders.
The mutation authority is the server/domain/persistence seam named in each row; the consumer column names the
downstream API, client surface, or release projection that must continue to agree with it.

The matrix is intentionally maintained as a current inventory. #706 routes all eighteen stable consumers through
the project-aware effective resolver and central client projection. Stored LegacyDefault configurations retain the
characterized compatibility marker while compiling behavior from their persisted graph; Active non-default
configurations are authoritative. A NonDefault Draft remains non-authoritative until the sole #713 activation gate
succeeds, and missing or malformed persisted authority fails closed.

Activation manifest source contract: `#702-legacy-consumers-v1` and the stable consumer identifiers in the
activation row below are the compiled readiness inventory; routed implementations register separately and cannot
flip a readiness flag in this document.
