# FMS Test Bench: source applicability matrix

*Stage A of the helicopter-first plan, 29 September 2026. Kept current as behaviour is built.*

The FMS Test Bench simulates a **rotorcraft CMA-9000**. This file records, for each behaviour, where the behaviour comes from, what it applies to, what the bench does, and where the bench deliberately differs. It replaces any single "which source wins" ladder: each row stands on its own source and applicability. The aircraft profile the bench flies is data in `product/client/src/fmsCdu/profile.ts` (`cma9000-s300-heli-civil`, version 6); its parameters, their basis and operational status are named in every run report. `configuration.ts` separately records target options, their implementation status and all 87 optional-function occurrences. Enabled target functions are not automatically implemented or qualified.

The bench is a demonstrator. Nothing here is an OEM conformance claim or qualification evidence.

The manuals are copyrighted. They are cited by page and paraphrased here; no manual text or image is in this repository.

**Borrowed versus laboratory.** For the autopilot, a *borrowed* value is a number taken from a cited AW189 description. The logic that sequences modes is laboratory unless a source gives it. The result is a representative generic rotorcraft autopilot informed by the AW189, not a claim about any installed controller.

## Default configuration

The default is the full civil SAR **target** adopted in [DEC-147](../../DECISIONS_AND_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md#dec-147---the-helicopter-bench-targets-a-full-civil-sar-configuration): ROTOR, civil navigation, RNP error limit, two CMA-5024 receivers, a radio altimeter and a representative rotorcraft AFCS. Search, mark, hover, PREDEF, moving rendezvous, flown-history backtrack, predictive RAIM, satellite deselection, dual FMS and RMS are configured. Their actual implementation status is recorded per option; the bench now runs two independent computers through a modeled link. Military navigation, military tactical approach, CARP/HARP, COSPAS/SARSAT, UHF/VUHF, EGI/IRS and Doppler are off. Other equipment-specific options carry individual dispositions rather than being inferred from the mission name. The civil CDU refuses the military tactical approach; the existing construction is retained only when explicitly configured in a laboratory profile.

The bank design and FMS command limit are the lesser of the selected profile's AFCS envelope and FMS roll-steering limit. Heading mode uses the AFCS envelope. Feed-forward/cross-track corrections do not add an extra five degrees outside either cap. The declared representative roll rate is 5 degrees/s. Fly-by anticipation includes a laboratory allowance for finite roll-in; holding entry reversals use the pattern's design bank so they do not silently fly a tighter radius than the prediction assumes. These are bench controller choices, not recovered OEM control laws. The controller reads its selected profile; declared-only acceptance allowances remain distinct from controller parameters.

The civil estimator consumes timestamped GPS, radio and air-data observations; only their bench generators receive aircraft truth. DR propagates the last measured position with heading, TAS and the last valid computed wind. Without valid air data it holds position, grows uncertainty and withdraws managed steering. A valid but integrity-rejected GPS position is retained with uncertainty when no approved alternative is available (M300 1-7); this grants neither approach nor hover authority. If radio is approved, the independent comparison and radio accuracy determine whether to retain that GPS or revert. The current bench approval table excludes radio updating during APPROACH; it is a conservative laboratory policy pending installation-specific approval data. NAV STATUS and sensor routing show the actual retained source and uncertainty.

Radio AUTO tunes up to six nearby uninhibited facilities from the estimated position. Measured slant ranges are corrected using air-data altitude, then solved using range circles; fewer than three usable facilities falls back to collocated VOR/DME (M300 1-8). Station elevation is currently zero, the local-plane solver is an approximation, and propagation/terrain masking and the OEM Kalman filter are absent. The 3-second acquisition, 0.02-NM range bias, 0.25-degree bearing bias, 30–150-degree crossing-angle window, 0.5-NM residual limit and 2-second sample-age limit are bench assumptions. DR uncertainty uses 2-kt wind, 0.5-kt TAS and 1-degree heading allowances; without air data it grows at 10 NM/h. Neither bound uses truth error.

GPS PREDICT RAIM displays IDENT/ETA and seven intervals from ETA minus 15 to plus 15 minutes, every five minutes (M300 5-27). Destination-airport requests use the selected approach's MAP; off-route points require entered ETA. APPR/TERM/ENRT/NONE reflect 0.3/1/2-NM HIL bands; `****` means prediction unavailable. The sky is simulated orbit geometry, visibly labelled; no real GPS almanac is loaded. SAT DESELECT excludes PRNs 1–32 from these predictions only (M300 5-28), leaving live receiver tracking unchanged. FMS startup resets these prediction exclusions. External sensor adapters currently provide no prediction almanac and show `****`.

`sensorPorts.ts` defines the internal simulator/replay mailbox and guidance output envelope (timestamp, sequence, NORMAL/NCD/FAIL). Replayed samples cannot replace accepted input, and stale or future-dated samples cannot navigate. These ports are not physical ARINC framing or proof of native CMA loading.

## Magnetic reference and initialization

The consumed magnetic database is NOAA **WMM2025**, epoch 2025.0, released 13 November 2024, with degree-12 coefficients and secular variation. `magvar.ts` ports the public-domain NOAA calculation and is compared with all 12 independent published field vectors at 2025.0/2027.5 and 0/100 km above the WGS84 ellipsoid (laboratory comparison tolerances 0.11 nT and 0.011 degree). The original coefficient SHA-256 is `06791cd95faba7bdf4a709808f2715a53fe689b29c23b9886bc2196fa9b3eb13`.

IDENT 2/2 and the preflight tools show model, epoch, release, validity and CRC. Export/load uses the bench's `aerolink-magvar-v1` JSON package: CRC-32Q covers the JSON tuple of format, model name, epoch, release and coefficient text. This is **not a CMA proprietary table**. Unsupported packages are refused without replacement; a recognized corrupted package is installed failed and withdraws FMS navigation with MAG VAR CRC FAILED / SYSTEM FAILED recall (M300 E-20). After the five-year boundary, MAG VAR OUT OF DATE is a power-up/load maintenance advisory (E-26). The bench continues secular-variation extrapolation outside the model's 2025–2030 validity; that extrapolation is not NOAA-qualified accuracy.

SETUP defaults to MAG and converts CDU courses, angular entries, generic EFIS/HUD headings and the crew heading selector; geometry and aircraft-state ports remain TRUE. Wind stays TRUE. Published facility variation is still used for imported magnetic bearings; a pilot PBD/PBPB bearing uses WMM at its reference position. Above N73 or below S60 the display forces TRUE, inhibits the toggle and issues USING TRUE REF when needed; leaving retains TRUE and issues CHECK TRUE/MAG REF (M300 3-8/3-9, A-188, E-31). Without measured GPS height above the ellipsoid, declination uses **0 km ellipsoid height**, not barometric/MSL altitude, as a declared bench approximation. The ideal GPS generator treats plant altitude as HAE without geoid separation; an external adapter supplies measured HAE. Independent aircraft heading remains explicitly TRUE on FMS failure.

Cold/warm FMS power is separate from GPS receiver power. The explicit bench ground input chooses the startup rule. A five-second white-screen/lamp test is a laboratory duration; startup resumes usable GPS or the last calculated position, retains cruise TAS, clears cruise wind only for a cold ground start and requires a crew active waypoint/leg after an airborne restart (M300 3-7/3-19, E-40). MOD and pending approach selections, facility inhibitions and predictive satellite deselections are cleared. Other persistent user/nav databases remain bench-retained; no unspecified OEM memory-retention claim is made. POS INIT refuses manual position/date/time while GPS is navigating. A simulated GPS UTC reference derives from the scenario clock; manual RTC edits drive UTC predictions, RAIM requests, rendezvous times and UTC alarms while sensor timestamps, elapsed startup tests and countdowns keep their scenario time base. No separate hardware UTC label, cockpit TRUE/MAG discrete or automatic weight-on-wheels sensor is asserted.

POS INIT 2/2 accepts manual QNH for fresh uncorrected pressure altitude, even without a runway, sharing the VNAV entry rule: 27.0–32.0 inHg / 915–1083 mb, with empty-entry unit toggle (M300 A-128). Crossing either departure boundary (>33 NM or >16,000 ft above departure elevation) cancels the departure setting; a later arrival setting is retained. Corrected ADC altitude hides the manual entry. Altitude correction remains the declared 27 ft/hPa laboratory approximation; external/hybrid QNH reference remains pending in the civil profile. The preflight links provide a crew review flow, not an automatic flight-ready determination.

## Two computers and civil radio management

`DualFmsSystem` runs two `ScriptedFms` computers and two CDU states against one aircraft. Each owns its page, scratch entry, MOD/ACT/secondary plan, sensor selection and local measured solution, power/fault state and guidance output. Only the selected flight controller integrates aircraft motion; the other computes against that same body. Two common physical GPS receiver models serve the bench; FMS 1 initially prefers GPS1 and FMS 2 GPS2. The inspected CDU and the selected AFCS/EFIS source are separate controls. Changing the AFCS source preserves the physical aircraft and crew basic-mode targets; captured approach guidance requires crew rearming as a declared laboratory policy. It is not an installed autopilot source selector.

M300 3-23/24 mode selection uses SETUP 5L, 6R confirmation or 6L cancellation. SYNC permits one route editor; the other gets !CDU ENTRY CONFLICT. EXEC transfers the computed plan, while the peer retains its own CDU state. Independent RTE 4L sends ACT (empty entry) or SEC into the receiver's MOD, and only the receiver's EXEC activates it. An existing receiving MOD is refused. Synchronization refuses lost communications/unavailable computer, program-policy or active navigation/user database mismatch, on-side active hold/GPS approach, any missed approach, and a pending MOD. Initiating-side settings and executed plan explicitly override the peer after an accepted synchronization. The extra MOD and crossfill user-database guards are fail-closed bench policies, not additional OEM rules. Link/power loss leaves independent operation; restoring a link/computer does not silently resynchronize.

Implemented M300 3-24/25 synchronization includes position initialization, entered UTC/date and UTC/LOCAL offset, the supported MAG/TRUE display selection, cycle selection, executed active/inactive route and its hold entry/exit edits, QNH, RNP, planning/flight-number/transition entries, predicted-RAIM satellite exclusions, temporary/user waypoints, fuel entries, missed-approach requests, alerts and matching message acknowledgements. Coordinate datum is fixed WGS84 and wind reference fixed TRUE. Installation-only discrete interfaces, alternate heading/TAS/altitude source fallback, radio self-test/DME scanning/dual-hold tuning, VOR AUTO/MANUAL controls and HSI sensitivity entries are not modeled. Per-computer fault logs retain local power/link conditions; a propagated alert is not a copied independent hardware fault. No blanket implementation of every item in the manual's synchronization list is asserted.

Synchronized navigation chooses the best eligible measured local solution without reading plant truth. Switching to the peer's identical sensor type requires at least the manual's 100 m ANP improvement. Each computer computes phase from its local position before system adoption; disagreement for more than 30 seconds drops SYNC (M300 3-25). Independent GPS/GPS positions separated by more than the declared laboratory 0.5 NM raise GPS-GPS POS DISAGREE (3-26). An integrity-rejected position does not gain approach authority by crossing the link. Computed wind is transferred with adopted navigation for subsequent measured DR propagation.

The separate `RadioManagementSystem` owns shared COM1/2, NAV1/2, ADF1/2 and ATC1/2 tuning devices. Either CDU can request tuning in either mode, even with cross-talk lost (M300 3-26/13-16). A request remains pending while the displayed active frequency/code remains the last acknowledged value; laboratory feedback delay is 0.25 seconds and failed-device timeout 2 seconds. A COM swap changes standby only after acknowledgement. Standby values cross-talk while the link is available and otherwise remain local. Device acknowledgement is a modeled port, not RF reception or an installed radio bus. NAV tuning does not manufacture a navigation measurement: civil AUTO station acquisition/ranging remains its separate measured adapter. Channel spacing, volume, transponder operating modes/replies, ADF bearings, discrete status and installation-specific control-loss applicability remain partial or pending in the option register.

## Provenance, validity, selection and engagement

These are kept distinct across the bench, in place of one advisory/selected/coupled label:

- **Data words** (radio height, position, velocities, deviations) carry **provenance** (which sensor or computation) and **validity** (NORMAL, NCD, FAIL).
- **Targets and datums** (selected altitude, hover height, speed, heading) record **who selected them**: crew, FMS or procedure.
- **Controller state** is reported per autopilot axis: engaged, armed and degraded modes. **Coupling** means an engaged axis mode consuming an FMS output.
- **Advisory** describes an FMS output that no engaged mode consumes.
